Angelos VIP Account Deletion

If you wish to delete your account and remove all personal data associated with your Angelos VIP profile, please submit the request form below.

What data gets deleted:

  • Your account credentials and authentication records.
  • Your profile details (Name, Email, Birthday).
  • Your user profile, active loyalty offers, and coupon usage history.

Requests submitted through this form are processed manually within 30 days.
